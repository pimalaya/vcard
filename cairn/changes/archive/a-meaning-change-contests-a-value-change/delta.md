## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A parameter that says what the value means contests it
Into merge. A change to `VALUE`, `LANGUAGE`, `CALSCALE`, `MEDIATYPE`, `PHONETIC`, `SCRIPT`, `ENCODING`, `CHARSET` or the bare 2.1 `QUOTED-PRINTABLE` collides with a value-level action on the other side, both ways, each pair reported, the left side's line standing. A parameter describing the thing a value names still merges beside it. A meaning action both sides made alike, and a removal the other side's update beats, contest nothing.

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Every change either lands or is reported
In merge: a set-valued field a meaning change contests holds the left side's items and is reported, rather than carrying both sides' additions and removals.
